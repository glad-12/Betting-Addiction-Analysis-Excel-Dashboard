# Betting-Addiction-Analysis-Excel-Dashboard
Excel dashboard analyzing sports betting behavior, addiction levels, financial impact, demographics, platforms, and regions to support responsible gambling decisions.
 # Betting Addiction Analysis Dashboard

## Introduction

This project analyzes survey responses from **2,000 respondents** to explore gambling and sports betting behavior across different demographic groups.

The dataset includes information on:

- Age group
- Gender
- Average weekly betting activity
- Average bet amount
- Monthly income
- Percentage of income spent on betting
- Hours spent betting per week
- Addiction level
- Most-used betting platform
- Financial debt status
- Work-life impact
- Relationship strain
- Awareness of betting risks
- Previous attempts to stop betting
- Region

The analysis was conducted to identify betting trends, measure the financial and social impact of gambling, and uncover patterns that can support responsible gambling initiatives and informed decision-making.

## Business Problem

Sports betting has become increasingly accessible through online platforms, raising concerns about gambling addiction, financial instability, and its effects on personal well-being.

Beyond financial consequences, excessive betting can also contribute to:

- Psychological and emotional distress
- Reduced workplace productivity
- Strained personal relationships
- Lower overall quality of life

This project aims to answer key business questions such as:

- Which betting platforms have the highest betting activity?
- Which age groups are most affected by gambling addiction?
- Which regions show the highest betting behavior?
- How does betting affect personal finances?
- Does awareness of betting risks reduce addiction?
- Which demographic groups require greater intervention?

The analysis seeks to:

- Measure betting behavior across demographic groups
- Identify high-risk betting platforms

- Evaluate the financial impact of betting

## Key Insights

### Platform Insights

- Some betting platforms recorded significantly higher weekly betting activity than others.
- Higher betting activity on certain platforms corresponded with increased addiction scores.

### Age Insights

- Certain age groups demonstrated noticeably higher addiction scores.
- These same groups also recorded the highest average weekly betting frequency, indicating increased exposure to gambling risks.

### Regional Insights

- Betting behavior varies across regions.
- Some regions consistently reported both higher addiction scores and greater betting frequency, suggesting geographical differences in gambling participation.

### Financial Insights

- Respondents spending a larger percentage of their monthly income on betting were more likely to experience financial debt.
- Higher betting expenditure appears strongly associated with financial instability.

### Awareness Insights

- Awareness of gambling risks alone does not necessarily prevent excessive betting.
- Some respondents who reported being aware of gambling risks still recorded high addiction scores and frequent betting behavior.

### Gender Insights

- Female respondents recorded the higher average addiction percentage within this dataset.
- This suggests that intervention programs should consider all demographic groups rather than focusing only on males.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen Responsible Gambling Programs

Betting companies should introduce personalized spending limits, betting reminders, and voluntary self-exclusion features for users with frequent betting activity.

### 2. Target High-Risk Age Groups

Educational campaigns should focus on age groups with the highest addiction scores through schools, universities, workplaces, and social media.

### 3. Expand Regional Awareness Campaigns

Regions with higher betting activity should receive additional financial literacy programs and responsible gambling awareness initiatives.

### 4. Improve Financial Protection

Users should be encouraged to set monthly betting budgets, with alerts triggered when betting expenditure exceeds a healthy percentage of monthly income.

### 5. Support Early Addiction Detection

Betting platforms should use behavioral monitoring to identify patterns associated with addiction and recommend support resources before severe financial consequences occur.

### 6. Promote Workplace Support

Organizations should provide confidential counseling and employee assistance programs for individuals whose work performance is affected by gambling.

### 7. Encourage Behavioral Intervention

Since awareness alone may not reduce addiction, campaigns should include behavioral support, counseling services, and accessible treatment programs.
